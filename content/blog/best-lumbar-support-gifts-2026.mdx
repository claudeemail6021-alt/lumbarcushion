## Gift-Giving Notes for Lumbar Support

**The positioning instruction is essential.** The most common reason lumbar cushions "don't work" is wrong positioning — placed at mid-back rather than L4-L5 (belt-line height). A handwritten note: "Position at your belt line, not your shoulder blades" can make the difference between a gift that changes someone's back pain and one that ends up unused.

**Chair type matters.** Mesh-back chairs grip straps differently than solid chair backs. Gaming chairs have different geometry than office chairs. If you're not sure of the chair type, the ComfiLife's universal strap system is the most adaptable.

**It takes a week to feel the difference.** Lumbar support works gradually — the muscles that have adapted to poor posture take time to relax. Including a note that "give it 5–7 days before judging" prevents the recipient from abandoning it after one session.
